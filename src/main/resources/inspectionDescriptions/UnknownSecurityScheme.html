Reports a @security naming a scheme getOpenApiSecurityDefinitions() never declares.

The generator writes the name under security: anyway, referring to something components.securitySchemes does not define. The specification validates and cannot be used to authenticate anything. One installation carried 848 such tags, naming four schemes nobody had declared.

By default the inspection stays quiet in a project that declares no schemes at all: such an application has evidently not taken the feature up, and painting every tag red teaches people to look past the plugin rather than at it.
